Power Apps Development

Canvas and model-driven business apps, built on Dataverse and integrated with the rest of your Microsoft estate.

What is Power Apps?

Power Apps lets you build custom business applications quickly, without a full custom-development project. There are two main types: canvas apps, which give you pixel-level control over the layout and connect to hundreds of data sources, and model-driven apps, which are generated from your data model in Dataverse and are well suited to complex, data-heavy business processes such as case management or CRM-style applications.

Our Power Apps development services

  • Canvas app design and development for line-of-business workflows
  • Model-driven apps built on Dataverse for structured business processes
  • Integration with Dynamics 365, SharePoint, Azure and third-party APIs
  • Migrating legacy Access/Excel-based tools onto the Power Platform
  • App governance, ALM (application lifecycle management) and ongoing support

When Power Apps is the right choice

If a team is running a business process from spreadsheets, email or a legacy Access database, that's usually a strong signal a Power App would remove manual effort and give you better data quality and reporting. We'll assess your process and tell you honestly whether Power Apps is the right fit — or whether a different part of the Power Platform, or a fuller Dynamics 365 CE implementation, would serve you better.
