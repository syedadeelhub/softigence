Power BI Consulting

Real-time dashboards and reporting connected directly to your Dynamics 365 and Power Platform data.

What is Power BI?

Power BI is Microsoft's business analytics platform. It connects to virtually any data source — Dynamics 365, Dataverse, SQL Server, Excel, Azure and more — and turns it into interactive dashboards and reports that update automatically as your underlying data changes.

Our Power BI consulting services

  • Dashboard and report design for leadership and operational teams
  • Data modelling and DAX measures on top of Dynamics 365 and Dataverse data
  • Power BI embedded analytics inside Power Apps and Power Pages
  • Automated refresh and data gateway configuration
  • Governance: workspaces, row-level security and licensing guidance

Why real-time reporting matters

Most organisations we work with start out consolidating reports manually in spreadsheets. That's slow, error-prone, and out of date the moment it's finished. Connecting Power BI directly to your source systems gives leadership a live, trustworthy view of the business, and frees up the people who were previously building reports by hand.
