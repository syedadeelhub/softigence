Dynamics 365 CE Integration

We design and build Dynamics 365 Customer Engagement (CE) implementations and integrations that connect your CRM to the rest of your Microsoft estate: Power Platform, Azure, SharePoint and third-party line-of-business systems. From data migration and custom entity design to plugin development and Azure Logic Apps/Power Automate integration patterns, we help you get a single, reliable view of your customers.

Case studies

City of Wolverhampton Council

Local Government

Problem: Disconnected case-handling tools across departments were slowing down citizen service delivery.

Solution: A Dynamics 365 CE case management implementation, integrated with Power Automate to route and track cases automatically.

Outcome: A single, shared view of citizen interactions and faster case resolution across departments.

Royal Academy of Engineering

Professional Membership Body

Problem: Membership and programme applications were managed through manual, spreadsheet-based processes.

Solution: A Power Apps model-driven app on Dataverse to manage membership and programme workflows, with Power BI reporting on top.

Outcome: Reduced administrative overhead and better visibility into programme performance.

Financial Ombudsman Service

Financial Services / Regulatory

Problem: Reporting on case volumes and outcomes required manual data consolidation across teams.

Solution: Power BI dashboards connected to core case data, kept current with Power Automate flows.

Outcome: Real-time visibility into case trends for leadership, with far less manual reporting effort.

Ultima Business Solutions

IT & Managed Services

Problem: Sales and service teams worked from disconnected tools with no shared customer record.

Solution: A Dynamics 365 CE implementation integrated with existing business systems via the Power Platform.

Outcome: A unified customer record and a smoother handoff between sales and service teams.

Supporting Humanity

Charity / Humanitarian Aid
