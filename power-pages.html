Power Pages Development

Secure, external-facing portals built on Dataverse and integrated with Dynamics 365.

What is Power Pages?

Power Pages (formerly Power Apps portals) lets you build secure, external-facing websites that read and write data directly to Dataverse, for example a citizen self-service portal, a supplier onboarding site, or a customer account area, without exposing your internal systems directly.

Our Power Pages development services

  • External-facing self-service portals backed by Dataverse
  • Secure authentication and role-based access for external users
  • Custom forms, lists and business process integration
  • Integration with Dynamics 365 CE case management
  • Ongoing support, security review and performance tuning

Typical use cases

Citizen or member self-service portals for local government and membership bodies, supplier and partner portals, and customer account areas that let external users check status or submit requests without a phone call or email to your team.
