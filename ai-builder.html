AI Builder Consulting

Practical AI: document processing, prediction models and automation, built into Power Apps and Power Automate.

What is AI Builder?

AI Builder brings pre-built and custom AI models into Power Apps and Power Automate, including document and form processing, prediction models, object detection and text analysis, without needing a dedicated data science team to get started.

Our AI Builder consulting services

  • Document and form processing to remove manual data entry
  • Prediction models built on your historical Dataverse data
  • Integrating AI Builder models into Power Apps and Power Automate flows
  • Guidance on when AI Builder is enough, versus when you need Azure AI services

A practical starting point

The organisations that get the most value from AI Builder usually start with a single, well-defined process. Processing incoming invoices or forms is a common first project, rather than a broad, open-ended "add AI" initiative. We'll help you identify that starting point.
