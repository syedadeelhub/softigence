What is the Microsoft Power Platform?

The Microsoft Power Platform is a suite of low-code tools (Power Apps, Power Automate, Power BI, Power Pages and AI Builder) built on the same Dataverse data platform as Dynamics 365. Used well, it lets organisations build custom apps, automate processes, report on data and expose secure external portals without a lengthy custom development cycle.
